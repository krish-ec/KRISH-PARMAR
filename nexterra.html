01 / Overview

A planning toy that feels like a real tool.

NexTerra is an urban heat intelligence planning demo. You drag cooling solutions from a toolbox of eight onto a schematic neighbourhood heat map, then flip between Current situation and After implementation.

It shows a baseline against an after temperature (the baseline is 39.2°C), plus co-benefits: CO₂e per year, energy per year, water retained and a comfort index. Demo cities are Bengaluru and Vadodara.

02 / Problem

Heat is hard to picture.

Heat planning usually lives in reports and static maps. It is hard to feel what a row of trees, a cool roof or a pond would actually change on a particular street.

The goal: make the trade-offs visible and playful enough that anyone can try them, while staying honest that the numbers are illustrative.

Demo data only. Nothing here is a live measurement.

04 / Process

From a hot map to a playable one.

  1. 01

    Frame the question

    What would someone want to try first on a hot street?

  2. 02

    Keep the map schematic

    A simple heat map reads faster than a detailed one.

  3. 03

    Make it playable

    Drag, drop, see the change. Levels and points reward trying more.

  4. 04

    Explain it

    A tour, a scripted assistant and an action plan turn play into a plan.
