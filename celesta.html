The problem

Event pages inform. They rarely make you feel it.

Most event pages list a date, a venue and a registration link. I wanted visitors to feel the atmosphere before they arrived, with essential details still easy to find. The scroll-driven ticket turns the page structure into the invitation itself.

The decisions

Drama where it counts. Clarity everywhere else.

A dark, nightlife-inspired palette with red accents and chrome type keeps the identity tight. Motion is saved for moments that matter: the ticket, the countdown and the reveal. Responsive layout and theme support keep the details readable on any screen.

What I learned: strong visuals earn attention, but structure and a clear next step earn the visit.
