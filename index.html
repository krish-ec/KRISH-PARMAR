01 / Hello, I’m Krish

Hi, I’m Krish.

I’m a Computer Science and Engineering student at GSFC University who likes building things people actually enjoy using. I care about the small details: the way a button moves, the way a page loads, the moment something feels alive.

I work across the whole stack, from interactive front-end with React and Three.js to Python and Django on the back. I like taking an idea from a rough sketch all the way to something people can open and use.
