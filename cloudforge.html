Memorising service names isn’t understanding.

Cloud learners often know what each service is called but not why a system fails. CloudForge puts them inside a simulated architecture (API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB) and asks them to read the evidence. The AI mentor nudges instead of handing over the answer, and quests, XP, badges and streaks make progress visible.
