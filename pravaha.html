Built around people

Culture grows when we show up together.

Pravaha brings communities, stories, and events into one place—then makes it easy to take part.

↗From shared interest to shared experience
The challenge

Bring culture online without losing its soul.

The goal was to turn a broad cultural ecosystem into a focused, emotionally engaging experience where people could discover events, connect with communities, and participate with ease.
The journey

Four steps from curious to connected.

0101 / DISCOVER

Find your people

Meet the communities shaping the culture.

0202 / EXPLORE

Follow a story

See what is happening and why it matters.

0303 / JOIN

Show up together

Turn interest into real participation.

0404 / SHARE

Keep it moving

Let every experience lead to another.

Approach

Design for emotion, structure for trust.

I focused on nature-inspired color, strong narrative rhythm, and a clean information hierarchy so the platform felt exciting but still easy to navigate. It had to feel energetic without being chaotic.
Product principle

Make the community the interface.

The experience is organized around people and shared moments, not a catalogue of disconnected events. That narrative gives the platform warmth while keeping the route to participation clear.

Culture is not content.
It is people showing up.

Pravaha puts community first, then gives every event, story, and shared moment a place to flow.
