The challenge

Make study tools feel motivating rather than overwhelming.

Students often struggle with cluttered dashboards and endless task lists. StudyNest needed to reduce noise so the user could focus on what mattered most, without losing momentum.
A day in StudyNest

Four calm moments.

08:30 / SET INTENTION

Choose one priority

Start with a clear, realistic goal for the day.

10:00 / FOCUS

Work in a sprint

Give one task your attention, then take a proper break.

14:00 / CHECK IN

See your progress

Adjust the plan without treating a change as failure.

18:00 / CLOSE OUT

Reflect and reset

Finish with a small win and a lighter tomorrow.

Approach

Reduce friction, increase momentum.

The design focused on clarity, calm pacing, and visible progress. Information was grouped into understandable layers so users could quickly see priorities and act with confidence.
